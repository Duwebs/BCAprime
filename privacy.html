Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

BCAPrime ("we", "our", "the app") is a free study library for BCA students — notes and previous year question papers (PYQs), organised by college and semester. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using https://bcaprime.vercel.app you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, display name and profile photo (when you sign in with email/OTP or Google).
  • Uploaded content — notes/PYQ files you submit, plus the title, subject, semester, college and uploader name you attach to them.
  • Google Drive data — if you use "Import from Google Drive", we request read-only access (drive.readonly) only to the file(s) you pick. We never read, list or store any other Drive files, and we never modify your Drive.
  • Analytics — anonymous usage events (page visits, searches, views/downloads counts, device type, OS, browser) to improve the app.
  • Push notification tokens — if you enable notifications, we store your subscription token to deliver alerts.
  • Local data — preferences (theme, college, semester, saved items, offline files) stored in your browser only.

2. How we use your data

  • To operate the library: show approved resources, counts, and your "My uploads" status.
  • To verify accounts and send OTP / welcome / notification emails.
  • To moderate uploads: your submissions go to our admins for review before becoming public.
  • To send push notifications you opted into (new material, approved uploads, senior requests).
  • To keep the platform safe and prevent abuse.

We never sell your personal data.

3. Google Drive — Limited Use

BCAPrime's use of information received from Google APIs (Google Drive) will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Drive access is read-only, is initiated only by you for files you explicitly select, and the imported file is used solely to create your upload.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database, file storage and push delivery.
  • Firebase (Google) — authentication.
  • Vercel — hosting and serverless functions (OTP / email delivery).
  • Resend — transactional email delivery.
  • Google APIs — sign-in and Drive file picker.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms.

5. Content you upload

Uploads are held privately for admin review. After approval they become publicly viewable and downloadable by all BCAPrime users, credited with the uploader name you provided. Only upload material you have the right to share.

6. Data retention & deletion

  • You can delete locally stored data anytime by clearing your browser storage.
  • To delete your account or your uploads, contact us — we will remove your data from our systems.
  • Push tokens are removed automatically when a subscription becomes invalid.

7. Children's privacy

BCAPrime is intended for college students. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, access-controlled databases (row-level security) and admin-only moderation. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data anytime by contacting us.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@bcaprime.com or use the Feedback option in the app.
